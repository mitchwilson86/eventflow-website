Common pricing questions

What does pricing look like?

Plans start at $199/month per venue with no per-email fees, plus a one-time $750 guided setup. You pay a flat monthly rate based on the level of autonomy you want. No long-term contracts required. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. All plans are month-to-month with no cancellation fees. You can pause, downgrade, or cancel at any time by emailing us. We'll export your data on request.

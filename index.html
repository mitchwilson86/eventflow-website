Friday, 7:42 PM. Every table full, your events manager on the floor, and a new inquiry in the events inbox.

Private-event inquiries rarely wait for a quiet hour, and the first venue to send a real answer usually gets the booking. EventFlow reads each inquiry the moment it lands in your events inbox, checks your calendar and your own venue facts, and replies from your address, signed the way your team signs. When something needs a person (a large buyout, a tour, a question it can't answer), it hands the guest to your events manager by name and tells you why.

Questions

How long does it take to set up EventFlow?

Most venues are up and running in about a week. We connect your events inbox, walk through your spaces, menus, and policies with you, and supervise the first week of replies. No technical work is needed on your side.

Does EventFlow integrate with my POS or CRM?

EventFlow works with Outlook (Microsoft 365), Slack, and Tripleseat today, and with Gmail on request. The Tripleseat integration is live with two-way sync: web leads answered automatically, availability checked against your calendar, and notes and holds written back. Custom integrations are available on the Premium plan.

How is my data kept secure?

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Each venue's knowledge base and guest data are kept separate, and no data is shared between clients. We never train AI models on your data.

What does pricing look like?

Plans start at $249/month per venue with no per-email fees, plus a one-time $750 guided setup. You pay a flat monthly rate based on the level of autonomy you want. No long-term contracts required. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime.

How accurate is the AI? Does it ever get things wrong?

EventFlow answers only from your venue's own knowledge base, and it checks key facts such as date availability before a reply goes out. No AI is perfect, so when it is not confident it saves a draft for your team to review, or hands the guest to a person, and tells you by email and Slack.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. All plans are month-to-month with no cancellation fees. You can pause, downgrade, or cancel at any time by emailing us. We'll export your data on request.
